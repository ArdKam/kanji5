## 2. Trademark screening

### Official search systems

WIPO Global Brand Database supports searches across international and participating national/regional collections and can search by keywords, names, goods/services and image similarity.

Source: https://www.wipo.int/en/web/global-brand-database

Japan's J-PlatPat trademark search supports mark/name searches, phonetic searches and goods/services similarity filtering.

Source: https://www.j-platpat.inpit.go.jp/t0100

USPTO guidance states that clearance should consider confusing similarity in sound, appearance, meaning and commercial impression, together with related goods/services, and that internet/common-law use also matters.

Sources:
- https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/search/likelihood-confusion
- https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/search/federal-trademark-searching

### Screening result

Web-indexed searches on 2026-10-02 did not surface an obvious exact-match RINEMI mark associated with Japanese-learning software/education.

Unrelated uses found include:
- RINEMI as an engineered-marble 3D wall/floor product name.
- OOO “RINEMI” as a Moscow-registered commercial entity.
- other unrelated personal/account uses of the string.

This does **not** establish registrability or freedom to operate.

### Recommended filing/search scope

At minimum, screening should cover the goods/services relevant to:
- downloadable/mobile software and related digital goods (Nice Class 9).
- education, training and language-learning services (Nice Class 41).

WIPO 2026 Nice Classification sources:
- Class 9: https://nclpub.wipo.int/esen/?class_number=9&lang=en&mode=flat
- Class 41: https://nclpub.wipo.int/esen/?class_number=41&lang=en&mode=flat

The search should not be restricted only to exact classes; USPTO guidance notes that related goods/services in different classes can still matter.

Decision: **preliminary screen complete; formal legal clearance remains external and mandatory before commercial launch.**
